# The TA/3D state words as a csbgen description; the winsys and the driver pack through it

`mesa/src/sgx545/csbgen/` describes the state words of `docs/STATE.md`
sections 2-5 in the XML read by Mesa's csbgen
(`src/imagination/csbgen/gen_pack_header.py`, the generator the PowerVR
Vulkan driver uses). meson generates a pack/unpack header for each file:

| file | prefix | words |
|---|---|---|
| `sgx545_vdm.xml` | `SGX545_VDMCTRL_` | state program (`PDS_STATE0/1`), the six draw words (`INDEX_LIST0`-`5`), stream terminate |
| `sgx545_ta.xml` | `SGX545_TA_` | TA state block: header, ISPA, ISPB, ISPC, PDS program words and word 1, region clip, viewport, output select, W clamp, MTE, TSP0/TSP1 |
| `sgx545_pbe.xml` | `SGX545_PBE_` | PBE words 0-3, the sideband word |
| `sgx545_tex.xml` | `SGX545_TEXSTATE_` | T0 (and strided T0), T1, T2, T3 |

`sgx545_csb.h` defines the address type and `sgx545_pack(dst, T, name) { ... }`,
the pack-in-place idiom of Mesa's `pvr_csb_pack`. The enums give names to what
STATE.md names: compare functions, stencil ops, object and pass types, cull,
the VDM primitives and block types, the PBE and texture formats, wrap and layout.

Where STATE.md gives a value but no meaning, the XML has a field named
`unknown_<bits>` with that value as its default, so a word's constant bits
come from the `_header` macro and not from the call site. Where STATE.md
gives no width, the field runs up to the next known field. Both kinds of
choice are listed as questions for STATE.md (below).

The hand-packed words are gone:

- **Winsys** (`sgx545_frame.c`):
  - the VDM stream: `vdm_state` for state programs, the six draw words, the
    terminate;
  - every TA state block: draws, the clear, the pass-type-3 scissor objects;
  - `pds_word` and the new `pds_sizeinfo`;
  - `ispa`, `mte_ctrl`;
  - the PBE words and sideband of the end-of-tile program;
  - the background object's texture words and PDS words.
- **Driver:**
  - `sgx545_tex_words`: T0-T3, including the render-target (strided) form;
  - `stencil_word`: ISPC, its op table mapped onto the XML's enum;
  - the vertex program's TSP words 0 and 1.

## Gaps in STATE.md

These went to the spec side as one list. In short:

- the VDM block type (the top nibble vs b29-31, since a fan's prim uses b28);
- the state info word's low bits (is the counter ORed or added? What are
  `0x2022400` and `0xe100`, field by field?);
- the field widths in the draw words, region clip, output select, PBE and
  TSP words;
- the meanings of every `unknown_*` field: draw b22-23, region clip b31,
  output select b11-13, MTE b2-31, PDS word 1 b16-17/b25-28, PBE word 2 b15
  and the sideband b9/b12, T0 b17/b19-26/b30, T3;
- PDS word 1 b25-28 for one unit with no secondaries: STATE.md now says 12,
  and the winsys writes 0xb (kept here, byte-identical);
- the strided T0 pitch: texels or 32-bit words? Do min/mag linear still apply?
- which byte order T1 formats 0x0c and 0x0d read (the names in STATE.md vs
  the driver's sampling).

## Not in this change

- The end-of-tile program's USSE words (`phas`, the LIMMs, the two EMITPIX)
  and the blend/colour-mask code are still hand-encoded. They are
  instructions, not state, and belong to the USSE encoder.
- The driver still reads TSP word 0 back by shifting (`varyings_match`); an
  unpack could replace that.
- §6 (the render context, PB, render details) is the kernel's.
