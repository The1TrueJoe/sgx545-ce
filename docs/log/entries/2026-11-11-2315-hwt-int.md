# hwt_int: the integer forms of the compiler's native_integers path, on hardware

2026-11-11, a CE5300 board on its normal boot (pvrsrvkm 1.7, SGX core rev
1.0.14), `tools/isa/hwt_open.py hwt_int` through `sgx_hwt`'s services backend,
under `/tmp/gpu.lock`. 55 tests, about 2 minutes, no recovery.

- 50 of 50 expected words. ima32's low word wraps, src2 negate subtracts (on
  an immediate too), `a * c56` is -a, its high word in i1 is the unsigned or
  (`.sgn`) signed product's. testmask with xor (eq, ne) and or (neg, pos, gt,
  le) writes ~0 / 0 dwords on the whole 32-bit result. test.xor and test.and
  set p0. and.inv with a register src2, xor.inv #0, asr and shl by a
  register. pck.f32.s16 / u16 pick the half by lane; pck.u16.f32 packs two
  values, rounded and clamped.
- frcp of 3, 7, 1000003, 4294967040 and 1.5 is the correctly rounded f32.
- ISA.md and COMPILER-GL3.md updated; raw output in
  `tools/isa/hwt_out/open/hwt_int.txt`.

The rig: `sgxinit` is not needed on the normal boot (the boot scripts
initialize the GPU; a second SrvInit returns 129, CONNECT_FAILED).
