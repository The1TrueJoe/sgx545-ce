# GL 3 driver run 3: ZLS layout (H9), depth/stencil through ZLS, baselines

2026-11-12, a CE5300 board, RAM boot of powervr_sgx's wip/msaa4-kcalc
4249037c (uAPI rev 3), open microkernel eb484b2 (features 0x2). Mesa from
claude/gl3-driver-850ecf 2fca2ad9 (A1 depth-only framebuffers and
framebuffer-sized renders, A3 depth/stencil through the ZLS buffer), x86_64
for dEQP, x86_64-gl for piglit and the tests, through drive-deqp.py with
`--boot ramboot`. The kernel image and the four modules were checked against
the build artifact before the run (md5 and `/proc/version`). The normal boot
was restored afterwards. No GPU resets, page faults, WARNs or BUGs in dmesg.

- **dEQP-GLES2**: 78 fail, 16638 pass, 445 skip, 4 warn: 0 differences from
  run 2 (e3201b0f).
- **Fixed-function piglit list**: 85 fail, 178 pass, 95 skip. 4 differences,
  all fail → pass (`copyteximage 1d`, `2d`).
- **GL 2.1 list**: 26 differences, all fail → pass:
  `depthstencil-default_fb-` `blit`, `drawpixels-24_8`, `readpixels-24_8`,
  `readpixels-float-and-ushort` at 1, 2 and 4 samples, and
  `gl-2.0-two-sided-stencil`. Still failing: `copypixels` and
  `drawpixels-float-and-ushort` at every sample count (st draws the depth
  through a gl_FragDepth shader, not written yet), `fbo-depthtex`,
  `fbo-alphatest-nocolor` and `-ff` (the window probe reads 0 0 0 0),
  `glsl-1.10-fragdepth`.
- **H9** (`mesa/tests/sgx_zls_layout.c`, `SGX_DEBUG=zlsdump`, read by
  `tools/zls_layout.py`): 10 cases, all FBOs complete. The ZLS buffer's
  order of docs/TEX.md section 7 fits every depth word and every stencil
  byte in every case: 1 and 4 samples, 96×64 and 120×72 (padded), 24-bit,
  16-bit (stored as float32), with stencil, and depth-only. No row-major or
  plain-tile alternative fits. Written into TEX.md section 7.
- **Depth/stencil gate list** (157 tests): the first chunk of 20 ran (11
  pass, 9 skip); then `piglit-runner` panicked (`lib.rs:1163`, an unwrap of
  `None`) and the rest didn't run. 94 of the 157 have results from the ff
  and GL 2.1 lists; the 63 FBO depth tests (`fbo-depth-*`,
  `fbo-depthstencil-*`) have none.
- **`sgx_msaa`**: all six cases pass at 720×480 and 256×256, the scaled
  resolve with `GL_SCALED_RESOLVE_FASTEST_EXT` included.
